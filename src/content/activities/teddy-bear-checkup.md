1. **Open the clinic.** Line up 2–4 stuffed patients; the child gets the doctor tools (spoon-thermometer, cup of medicine, scarf bandages).
2. **Hear the complaint.** You play the worried owner: "Doctor, Bunny won't eat his carrots!"
3. **Run the check-up.** Listen to the heart, check the temperature, look at ears and tummy — the doctor announces the diagnosis.
4. **Treat and bandage.** Wrap the paw, pour the pretend medicine, prescribe "three cuddles and a nap".
5. **Next patient!** Rotate through the waiting room until every animal is on the mend.
