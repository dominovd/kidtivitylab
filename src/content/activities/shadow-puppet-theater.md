1. **Darken the room and aim a flashlight at a blank wall.**
2. **Start with the classics.** A barking dog (thumb as jaw), a flying bird (linked thumbs), a wolf.
3. **Tell a story.** The bird meets the wolf... your child decides what happens next.
4. **Trade jobs.** They do puppets while you hold the light, then switch — holding the light steady is its own skill.
