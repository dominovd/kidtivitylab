1. **Gather the dirty crew.** Muddy pumpkins from the patch, garden or a strategic roll in the flowerbed.
2. **Open the station.** Shallow warm soapy tub, brush, drying towel laid out like a production line.
3. **Demonstrate the cycle.** Dunk, scrub, rinse, dry, place on the clean towel. One pumpkin, slowly.
4. **Hand over the shift.** The washer works the queue; you admire mud removal.
5. **Promote the fleet.** Clean pumpkins line the windowsill, decor by the in-house cleaning department, maintained weekly.
