1. **Draw the path.** Use a pencil to sketch a winding line of 20 to 30 squares from START to FINISH. Let your child trace over it in marker and write the numbers in each square.
2. **Add special squares.** Pick three or four squares and decide together what they do: slide back 2, jump ahead 3, do 5 jumping jacks, tell a joke. Draw a small symbol in each one.
3. **Choose your pieces.** Each player picks a small toy. Put them on START.
4. **Say the rules out loud.** Youngest goes first, then turns go around the table. A special square works once per turn, so landing on one after a jump does not trigger another. You reach FINISH with any roll that gets you there or past it. Ask your child to repeat the rules back.
5. **Play.** Roll the die, count the dots together, then move that many squares, counting each one out loud as the piece lands.
6. **Talk about the ending.** When someone wins, shake hands and say "good game." If your child loses, name the feeling and remind them there is always another round.
