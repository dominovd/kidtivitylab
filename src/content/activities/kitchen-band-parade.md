1. **Hand out instruments.** A pot drum, a spoon pair, a sealed rice shaker.
2. **Warm up.** Loud! Quiet! Fast! Slooooow! (This is secretly a self-control game.)
3. **March.** Lead a parade through the whole home, everyone playing their part.
4. **Big finale.** Count down 3-2-1... CRASH! Then applause and a bow.
