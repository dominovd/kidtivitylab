1. **Brew the pumpkins.** Baking soda + splash of water + orange coloring, packed into molds, frozen overnight.
2. **Set the cauldron.** Big bowl in a rimmed tray, vinegar loaded in squeeze bottles.
3. **Drop the first pumpkin.** Into the cauldron it goes — predictions, please.
4. **Squeeze the magic.** Vinegar hits, potion erupts, witch cackling optional but encouraged.
5. **Volcano finale.** The last cup of loose baking soda gets the full vinegar pour — biggest fizz wins bedtime stories.
