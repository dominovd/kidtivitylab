1. **Make the frame.** An adult cuts a frame shape from cardboard and covers the border with double-sided tape or tape loops, sticky side up.
2. **Head outside.** Bring a basket and walk a park, trail or yard, looking for leaves, petals, twigs and grass.
3. **Ask before picking.** Look first, ask before picking, and leave mushrooms, berries and anything unfamiliar alone.
4. **Press finds onto the tape.** Hand over the frame and let your child stick each piece down, naming it as they go.
5. **Fill the border.** Keep collecting until the sticky edge is covered. Overlapping petals and leaves look best.
6. **Wash hands and hang it up.** Rinse hands, then stand the frame on a shelf or tape it to a window as a reminder of the walk.
