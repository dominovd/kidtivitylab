1. **Roll the ammo.** Turn 6–10 pairs of socks into snowballs.
2. **Choose your game.** Free-for-all snowball fight, or target practice into the laundry basket.
3. **Set one rule.** Head shots are out; below the shoulders only.
4. **Play rounds.** Loser collects the socks — which is secretly the tidying-up.
