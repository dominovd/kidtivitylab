1. **Raid the recycling.** Collect 4–6 clean jars and bottles with their caps.
2. **Set the tray.** Line them up, caps loosely on, biggest to smallest.
3. **Demonstrate one twist.** Slow wrist turns, exaggerated, silent — let the movement speak.
4. **Hand over the station.** Off, on, swap, shuffle — follow their lead.
5. **End with a match-up.** Shuffle all caps and restore order together, then the station stacks away for tomorrow.
