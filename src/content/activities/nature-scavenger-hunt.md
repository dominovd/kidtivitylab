1. **Make the list together.** 6–10 items: something round, something rough, two kinds of leaves, something yellow, something that smells good.
2. **Set the boundaries.** "Anywhere in the yard" or "between here and the big tree".
3. **Hunt.** Collect what's collectible; photograph or just point at what isn't (no picking flowers from the neighbor's garden).
4. **Show and tell.** Lay out the treasures and let them present each find — this half is where the learning is.
