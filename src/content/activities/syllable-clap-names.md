1. **Pick a name.** Start with your child's own name. Say it slowly and stretched: "Ma-ya."
2. **Clap each beat.** Clap once for every part as you say it together. Count the claps out loud: "Two claps!"
3. **Take turns.** Your child picks a name, says it slowly, and claps. You guess the count before they clap.
4. **Change the category.** Move from names to foods, animals, or toys. "Ba-na-na. How many claps?"
5. **Add movement.** Stomp, jump, or tap knees for each beat instead of clapping.
6. **Sort into piles.** Make spots for one, two and three claps, then sort toys or snacks into the right spot.
