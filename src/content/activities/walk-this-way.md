1. **Pick a path.** Choose a hallway, a loop through the living room, or a stretch of yard.
2. **Call a situation.** "Walk as if you're sneaking up on someone!"
3. **Everyone walks it.** Give each prompt 10 to 20 seconds, then call the next one.
4. **Switch speeds.** Alternate fast and slow, heavy and light: hot sand, then tiptoes past a sleeping baby.
5. **Pass the job.** Let each child take a turn calling out the prompts.
6. **Finish slow.** End with a calm prompt, like walking on clouds, to settle everyone down.
