1. **Dump the socks.** Tip a basket of clean socks onto a bed or table so the whole pile is spread out and easy to see.
2. **Find the pairs.** Ask your child to lay matching socks side by side by color and pattern. Say the rule out loud: "Same color, same stripes, that's a pair."
3. **Roll each pair.** Show how to stack two socks flat, then roll from the toe and tuck the top. Let your child do the rest.
4. **Sort into family piles.** Make a pile for each person, biggest feet to smallest. Ask "Whose pile is this?" as they go.
5. **Count the pairs.** Count out loud together. Point out any sock with no partner and set it aside in an odd sock pile.
6. **Add a challenge or a chart.** Race a two-minute timer for fun, or add a sticker to a paper chart if this is a weekly job.
