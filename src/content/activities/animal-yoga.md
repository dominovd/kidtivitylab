1. **Open the zoo.** Mats or towels down, shoes and socks off, one adult as head zookeeper.
2. **Wake the animals.** Cat stretch on all fours: back up, back down, slow.
3. **Visit the loud ones.** Frog jumps, bear walks, lion's breath with the biggest roar allowed indoors.
4. **Slow the safari.** Flamingo balance, butterfly sit, snake stretch — voices dropping with the pace.
5. **Put the zoo to sleep.** Sleeping swan (child's pose) with three bee-hum breaths, then whisper the animals goodnight.
