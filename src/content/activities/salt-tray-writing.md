1. **Set up the tray.** Lay a towel on the table, set the baking sheet on it, and pour in a thin layer of salt, about 1/8 inch. Lay colored paper on the bottom first if you want letters to pop.
2. **Start with shapes.** Ask your child to draw a line, a circle, then a zigzag with one finger. Say the shape name as they go.
3. **Wiggle to erase.** Keep the tray on the table and slide it gently side to side until the salt is even again. Erase between every drawing.
4. **Move to letters.** Draw one letter from their name in the salt and ask them to guess it. Then let them copy it from the card.
5. **Swap turns.** They draw a letter for you to guess, and you say its sound out loud.
6. **Try a tool, then tidy up.** Let them write a few letters with a paintbrush or chopstick as a pencil warm-up. Pour the salt back into a container for next time and wash hands.
