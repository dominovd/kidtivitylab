1. **Plan the trail.** Pick 5–8 hiding spots forming a route through the home.
2. **Draw the clues.** On each paper, sketch the place where the next clue waits. Clue #1 stays in your hand; the treasure sits at the final spot.
3. **Hide them in reverse.** Start from the treasure and work backwards — the only way to not confuse yourself.
4. **Hand over clue #1.** Then follow the expedition, narrating discoveries and rationing hints.
5. **Celebrate the find.** The treasure is shared, the drawings become fridge art, and "again!" is guaranteed.
