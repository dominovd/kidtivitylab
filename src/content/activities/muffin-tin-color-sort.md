1. **Set out the muffin tin and a pile of colorful bits.**
2. **Drop one item in, naming the color.** "Red goes here!"
3. **Let them take over.** Resist correcting — sorting "wrong" is still sorting.
4. **Count the results together.** "Four blue, two yellow — blue wins!"
