1. **Draw the course.** A wavy line to walk like a tightrope, circles to hop between, a spiral to spin through, footprints to match.
2. **Add instructions.** Arrows, "JUMP!", a lightning bolt for "run fast".
3. **Run it.** Time each run, or just keep going until legs are tired.
4. **Redesign.** Hand over the chalk and run THEIR course — expect it to be harder than yours.
