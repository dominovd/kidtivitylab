1. **Set up the tub.** Put a shallow tub on flat ground, add a couple of inches of water, and set the cups and ladle beside it.
2. **Show one slow pour.** Ladle water into a cup, then tip it into a bowl while your child watches.
3. **Hand over the ladle.** Let them try. Stay close and let them miss, spill and try again.
4. **Add containers as interest dips.** Swap in a funnel, a sponge or a bigger bowl to keep the game fresh.
5. **Tip it out at the end.** Empty the tub together, dry hands, and carry the containers back inside.
