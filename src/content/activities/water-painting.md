1. **Fill a bucket with plain water.** That's the whole setup.
2. **Hand over a real paintbrush.** Wall-painting brushes feel important and build arm strength.
3. **Paint everything.** Fence, steps, sidewalk, the dog's water bowl.
4. **Watch it disappear.** Sun-dried "paint" means the canvas resets itself — this can run on its own for a surprisingly long time.
