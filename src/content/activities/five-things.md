1. **Call a category.** "Five things that fly!"
2. **Count on fingers.** The player names answers, raising a finger per answer, everyone listening.
3. **Hit five, celebrate.** A cheer, a drumroll on the seat, whatever your car does.
4. **Rotate the caller.** Whoever answered picks the next category and target player.
5. **Escalate weirdness.** As rounds pass, categories drift from 'animals' to 'things that would be terrible pillows' — that's the game working as designed.
