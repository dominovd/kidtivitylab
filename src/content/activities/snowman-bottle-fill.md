1. **Give the bottle a face.** Eyes, carrot nose, smile, buttons — a naked snowman waiting for snow.
2. **Set out the snow.** Cotton balls in a bowl beside the bottle.
3. **Demonstrate two drops.** Slowly, with a satisfied "in!"
4. **Hand over the job.** Ball by ball, the snowman fills; count along if numbers are the current obsession.
5. **Blizzard finale.** Full snowman gets admired, shaken, then dumped out — and the storm begins again.
