1. **Load the stocking.** 5–8 familiar objects go in while the guesser looks away.
2. **Reach, don't peek.** One hand in, eyes on the ceiling.
3. **Describe first.** Hard or soft? Big or small? Cold? Bumpy?
4. **Guess and reveal.** Name it, pull it out, celebrate or laugh — then next object.
5. **Swap roles.** The child restocks the stocking and enjoys absolute power over your guessing.
