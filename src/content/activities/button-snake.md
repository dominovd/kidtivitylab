1. **Make the snake base.** Sew a large button to one end of the ribbon and tie a firm knot at the other end.
2. **Cut the felt squares.** Make 8 to 10 squares, 2 to 3 inches wide, each with a slit in the middle.
3. **Show the motion once.** Push the button through one slit and slide the square down the ribbon, then hand it over.
4. **Let them build.** Your child pushes the button through each square and slides them along, making a long snake.
5. **Take it apart.** Unbutton every square back off, then start again.
6. **Bridge to real clothes.** When they're ready, lay a cardigan flat and let them button the bottom button first.
