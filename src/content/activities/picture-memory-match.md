1. **Make the cards.** Cut 6 index cards in half to get 12 cards, then draw one simple picture on each pair: two suns, two apples, two houses. Stickers work just as well.
2. **Shuffle and lay out a grid.** Mix the cards face down and arrange them in rows, 3 across and 4 down, so every card is visible.
3. **Take turns flipping two.** Player one turns over two cards. If they match, they keep the pair and go again. If not, flip them back and pass the turn.
4. **Say the pictures out loud.** Name each card as you flip it: "Sun and tree, no match." Naming helps kids hold the pictures in mind.
5. **Play until the grid is empty.** Count pairs at the end. Everyone wins something, and the child who found the most gets to shuffle for the next round.
6. **Add pairs next time.** Once 6 pairs feels easy, grow the grid to 8 or 10 pairs for a longer, trickier game.
