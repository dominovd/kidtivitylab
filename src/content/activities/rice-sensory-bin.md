1. **Set the scene.** Sheet on the floor, bin on the sheet, rice in the bin.
2. **Open with hands only.** Let them plunge, sift and pour with fingers first — this is the core of the experience.
3. **Add tools.** Cups, spoons, a funnel if you have one. Show one slow pour and step back.
4. **Bury treasure.** Slip a few toys under the surface and announce a dig.
5. **Close with the cleanup game.** "Every grain back in the box" is scooping practice disguised as tidying, and the bag goes away until next time.
