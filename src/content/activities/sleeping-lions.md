1. **Lions lie down.** Everyone finds a spot, closes their eyes, and goes completely still.
2. **The hunter prowls.** One player (start with a grown-up) walks among the lions trying to make them move or laugh — talking, telling jokes, being absurd. No touching, ever.
3. **Spotted lions join the hunt.** Any lion caught moving becomes a hunter too.
4. **Last lion wins.** The stillest lion becomes the next hunter — or, before cake, end with every lion "asleep" and whisper the next instruction.
