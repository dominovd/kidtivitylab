1. **Set the boundaries.** Name where you can hide and where you can't.
2. **Pick a seeker.** The seeker covers their eyes at home base and counts out loud.
3. **Everyone hides.** Hiders find a spot before the count ends and stay quiet.
4. **"Ready or not, here I come!"** The seeker calls it out and starts searching.
5. **Find everyone.** Found players can help search or wait at home base.
6. **Switch roles.** The last person found becomes the next seeker.
