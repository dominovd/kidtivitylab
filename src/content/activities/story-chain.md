1. **Someone starts.** "Once upon a time, a very hungry dinosaur woke up in our garage..."
2. **Next person adds ONE sentence.** Then pass it on.
3. **Keep the chain going.** The only rule: build on what came before — no "that didn't happen".
4. **Land the ending.** When it peaks, someone says "...and that's why we never park in the garage. The end."
