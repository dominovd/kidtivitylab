1. **Load the bag.** Slide the paper in flat, then add blobs of 2–3 paint colors, spaced apart.
2. **Seal it tight.** Squeeze the air out, zip it shut, and tape over the seal and edges.
3. **Fix it down.** Tape the bag to the floor, tray, table, or a low window.
4. **Hand over the canvas.** Show one squish, then let fingers, palms, and fists do the rest.
5. **Save it if you like.** An adult can open the bag over a protected surface and leave the paper flat to dry; otherwise, keep the bag sealed and discard it.
