1. **Anchor the pumpkin.** Flat side down, wedged so it cannot roll.
2. **Set the first tee.** One adult tap plants it; explain the helping-hand rule.
3. **Hammer time.** Tap, tap, THUNK — the tee sinks flush. Next tee.
4. **Follow the face.** Older kids hammer along a drawn jack-o-lantern outline.
5. **Harvest the tees.** Pulling them all back out is gripping work — and resets the game for tomorrow.
