1. **Pick the spot.** Between the couch and two chairs is the classic.
2. **Build the roof.** Drape blankets over the furniture; weigh down corners with books or cushions.
3. **Furnish the den.** Pillows on the floor, flashlight hanging or propped, book stack in the corner.
4. **Move in.** Read together by flashlight, or let them have solo den time — both are wins.
