1. **Inflate the balloon.** Blow up one balloon (plus a spare — trust us).
2. **Set the rule.** The balloon must never touch the floor. Any body part may hit it.
3. **Count aloud.** Every hit adds one — the group's score, not any single player's.
4. **Beat your record.** When it drops, laugh, announce the streak, and start again.
