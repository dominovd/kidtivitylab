1. **Stuff pom-poms between the whisk wires.** Fill it generously.
2. **Hand it over.** Show one rescue, then let them work.
3. **Stay close and watch.** This is a supervised activity, but a hands-off one — the struggle is the point.
4. **Reload and repeat.** When rescuing gets easy, switch roles: they load, you "struggle".
