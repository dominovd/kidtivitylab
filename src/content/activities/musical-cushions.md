1. **Set the circle.** One cushion per child, spread in a wide ring.
2. **Music on — move.** Everyone dances or marches around the circle.
3. **Music off — land.** Every player must touch a cushion. Easy round one: one each.
4. **Remove a cushion, not a child.** Each round, take one cushion away. Players must now share.
5. **End in a heap.** The finale: the whole group balanced on the last cushion or two, laughing too hard to stand.
