1. **Fill the tub.** A few cups of colored rice or shredded paper as the snow.
2. **Bury the treasure.** Bells, bows and tiny boxes disappear under the surface.
3. **Open the dig.** Hands first: sift, find, ring, hide again.
4. **Add tools.** Cups and scoops arrive as chapter two.
5. **Shake-sheet cleanup.** Everything back in the tub, lid on — it keeps until tomorrow's session.
