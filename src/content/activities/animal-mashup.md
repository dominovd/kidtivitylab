1. **Gather the zoo.** Players spread out in the cleared space; one person starts as the caller.
2. **Call two animals.** "Move like a... CRAB. Sound like a... ROOSTER!"
3. **Mash them up.** Everyone crab-walks around crowing, until the caller says stop (30–60 seconds is plenty).
4. **Pass the calling.** New caller, new absurd combo. Snake that quacks, elephant that squeaks, penguin that roars.
5. **Finish slowly.** End with a quiet combination — perhaps a sleepy sloth humming like a bee — if you need to bring the energy back down.
