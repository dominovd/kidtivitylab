1. **Set up the water and the chart.** Fill a clear bin or the sink with a few inches of water. Draw two columns on paper, one for sink and one for float.
2. **Line up the objects.** Put all 10 in a row: metal spoon, cork, apple, coin, leaf, crayon, sponge, toy car, plastic lid, rock.
3. **Guess first.** Your child holds one object and says "sink" or "float" before it goes in. Write or draw the guess on the chart if you like.
4. **Drop and watch.** Let them drop it in and watch what happens. Then move it to the matching column on the chart.
5. **Talk about the surprises.** The apple floats, the coin sinks. Ask "did that surprise you?" and let them try a reason.
6. **Try the extra round.** See if they can make the coin float on a plastic lid boat, or squeeze water into the sponge underwater and see if it still floats. Empty the water right after.
