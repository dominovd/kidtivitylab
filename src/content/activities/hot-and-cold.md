1. **Set the rules.** Walk the room and name the no-go spots: oven, high shelves, stairs, and anywhere outside the house.
2. **Send the seeker out.** Have them wait in the hallway or face a wall and count to ten while the toy gets hidden.
3. **Hide the toy.** Keep it in the same room, somewhere a small hand can reach.
4. **Guide with four words.** The hider says freezing, colder, warmer, or hot as the seeker moves around. Nothing else.
5. **Celebrate and swap.** Cheer when the toy is found, then trade roles so the child gets to hide and give clues.
6. **Make it harder.** Once it feels easy, try the clapping version or hide three items in a row.
