1. **Choose the rescue zone.** A clear stretch of wall, the high chair tray, or a plastic tray — anywhere tape sticks and your child can stand or sit comfortably.
2. **Tape the toys down.** One or two strips of painter's tape across each toy. Leave a corner of tape lifted for beginners.
3. **Sound the alarm.** "Oh no — the animals are stuck! Can you rescue them?" Show one rescue slowly, then hand over the job.
4. **Rescue and collect.** Each freed toy goes into the basket. Peeled tape goes to you (or gets balled up — also fun).
5. **Restick and repeat.** The same setup runs several rounds — most toddlers immediately ask for the toys to be trapped again.
