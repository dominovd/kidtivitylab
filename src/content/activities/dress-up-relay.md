1. **Build two piles.** At the far end of the room or yard, set out a button-up shirt, a zip jacket and a hat for each team. Keep the piles a few feet apart.
2. **Practice once, no timer.** Walk everyone through the order: shirt, jacket, hat. Let them try the buttons and zipper slowly so the race isn't the first time they've met them.
3. **Line up and send the first runner.** On "Go," the first player runs to the pile, puts on everything, then runs back.
4. **Undress and tag the next player.** Back at the start line, they take it all off and high-five the next teammate, who runs out.
5. **Cheer the last runner home.** The team whose last player gets back first wins. Swap piles between rounds so both teams get the same clothes.
6. **Finish with a solo time trial.** Anyone who wants a turn alone races the clock and tries to beat their own time.
