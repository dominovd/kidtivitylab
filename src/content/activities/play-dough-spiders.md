1. **Roll the body.** A dough ball between both palms, golf ball to fist-sized.
2. **Stab in the legs.** Eight pipe cleaner legs, counted aloud, four per side.
3. **Bring it alive.** Two googly eyes pressed in, instant personality.
4. **Bend the legs.** Shape each leg so the spider can stand on the tray.
5. **Start a story.** Give the spider a name, build a paper web, or make a smaller spider friend.
