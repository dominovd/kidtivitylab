1. **Fill the basket.** Gather 10 to 12 small objects that make rhyming pairs and drop them in a basket.
2. **Pull one out.** Your child picks an object, says its name out loud, and you repeat it together.
3. **Hunt for the match.** They search the basket for the object that rhymes, like finding the rock to go with the sock.
4. **Line up the pairs.** Set each matched pair side by side on the table so your child can see the growing row.
5. **Flip the game.** Call out a word and let your child find an object that rhymes with it.
6. **Get silly.** Take turns inventing nonsense rhymes: sock, lock, mock, zock.
