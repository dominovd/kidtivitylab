1. **Cut the stamps.** Slice a sponge into chunky triangles, one per paint color.
2. **Dip and press.** Sponge into the green paint, press onto paper, lift straight up — a tree!
3. **Grow the forest.** Stamp trees across the page until the artist declares it done.
4. **Dry break.** Let the forest dry — snack, nap, or day two.
5. **Decorate.** Stickers, sequins and pom-pom ornaments on every tree, then straight to the fridge gallery.
