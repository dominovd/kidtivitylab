1. **Print the cast.** 5–10 photos: family members, pets, the child's own things.
2. **Build the book.** Glue one photo per page of folded construction paper or slot them into a small album.
3. **Add the flaps.** One sticky note over each photo.
4. **Read it together.** "Who's hiding here?" — lift, reveal, name, react. Repeat every page.
5. **Hand over the flaps.** Soon the child runs the reveals and you do the guessing — deliberately, hilariously wrong.
