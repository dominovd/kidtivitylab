1. **Mark a start and a finish.** Two cushions, two chalk lines — anything works.
2. **Pick an animal.** Bear (all fours), frog (jumps), crab (belly up), penguin (waddle), snake (wiggle).
3. **Race!** Everyone crosses the room as that animal. Parent participation is not optional — that's the fun.
4. **Let your child invent one.** A "flying spaghetti walk" is a legitimate animal.
