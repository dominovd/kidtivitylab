1. **Fill the bottle 3/4 with warm water.**
2. **Add glue and glitter.** Roughly one part glue to three parts water, plus a generous glitter pour.
3. **Seal it seriously.** Cap on tight, then tape.
4. **Shake and watch together.** "Can you breathe slowly until the last glitter lands?" — that's the whole magic trick.
