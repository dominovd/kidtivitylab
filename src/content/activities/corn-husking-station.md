1. **Set the station.** Corn, two bowls, towel, a real workbench.
2. **Start the rip.** Loosen the first leaf, grip together, pull down slowly. RRRIP.
3. **Strip it bare.** Leaf by leaf into the husk bowl, then the fuzzy silk, the weirdest part, save it.
4. **Inspect the treasure.** Count rows, feel the kernels, smell it, this is dinner in its wrapper.
5. **Finish the job.** Wash the cobs, cook them tonight, and serve the proud farmer their own harvest.
