1. **Write the name big.** Use a marker on cardstock. Capital first letter, lowercase after, with a wide gap between each letter.
2. **Make a model.** Write the same name on a second strip and keep it on the table as a guide.
3. **Cut between the letters.** Hand over child-safe scissors and let your child cut along the gaps. Stay close and help steady the paper.
4. **Build it back.** Mix up the tiles and have your child line them up to spell the name, checking the model strip as needed.
5. **Try it without the model.** Once it feels easy, put the model strip aside and see if they can build the name from memory.
6. **Trace and copy.** Trace each letter with a finger, then copy two or three letters with a crayon on a fresh sheet.
