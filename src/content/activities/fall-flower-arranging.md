1. **Set the florist bench.** Tray, vase, flowers on the left, little jug of water, cloth.
2. **Pour the water.** Small jug into vase neck — the trickiest, proudest step.
3. **Place the flowers.** One stem at a time; when it won't fit, the puzzle begins.
4. **Adjust and admire.** Step back, look with the eyes, smell with the nose — florist's ritual.
5. **Deliver the masterpiece.** The vase takes its place on the family table, maintained by its maker till the next bouquet.
