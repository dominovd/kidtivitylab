1. **Wrap the parcel.** A small prize in the center, then one layer of paper per player — a tiny treat tucked into each layer.
2. **Circle up.** Everyone sits in a ring; the parcel starts with the birthday child or the youngest.
3. **Music on — pass.** The parcel travels around the circle hand to hand.
4. **Music off — unwrap.** Whoever holds it peels exactly one layer and keeps whatever it hides.
5. **Steer to the finale.** Pause fairly so everyone unwraps once; the last layer reveals the prize.
