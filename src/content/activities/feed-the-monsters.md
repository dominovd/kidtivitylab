1. **Build the monsters.** Glue a colored paper face onto each bag, cut a big mouth hole through both, decorate with goofy features.
2. **Open the restaurant.** Monsters lined up, mixed pom-poms in one bowl.
3. **Announce the rule.** Each monster only eats its own color — demonstrate one feeding with chomping sound effects.
4. **Feeding frenzy.** The child sorts snack by snack; monsters say thank you in silly voices.
5. **Check the bellies.** Open each bag and count what it ate — the built-in answer key to the sorting.
