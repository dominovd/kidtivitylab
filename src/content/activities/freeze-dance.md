1. **Clear the floor.** Push chairs back and define the dance zone.
2. **Start the music.** Everyone dances — the sillier the better.
3. **Pause it.** When the music stops, every dancer freezes like a statue.
4. **Inspect the statues.** Walk around admiring the poses; wobblers just laugh and reset.
5. **Repeat.** Keep rounds short and vary how long the music plays so nobody can predict the freeze.
