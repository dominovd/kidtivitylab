1. **Freeze the toys.** Place small washable toys in an ice cube tray or bowl, cover with water, and freeze until solid (about 3 hours — do this in the morning or the night before).
2. **Set up the rescue station.** A tray or towel, a bowl of warm water, a little dish of salt, and a dropper or spoon.
3. **Add warm water.** Show how to drip warm water onto the ice and watch it carve tunnels.
4. **Sprinkle on salt.** Salt melts the ice faster — talk about what's happening.
5. **Keep rescuing until every toy is free.** Cheer each rescue!
