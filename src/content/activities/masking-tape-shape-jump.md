1. **Tape 4–5 big shapes on the floor.** Circle, square, triangle, star — each about a step apart.
2. **Call a shape.** "Jump to the CIRCLE!"
3. **Speed up.** Faster calls, sillier voices, add colors or "hop on one foot".
4. **Swap the caller.** Being the boss is the best part — let them run you around.
