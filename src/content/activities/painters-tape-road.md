1. **Stick a winding road across the floor.** Two parallel lines of tape, about a hand's width apart. Curves are more fun than straights.
2. **Add destinations.** A block tower "gas station", a book "mountain", a cushion "car wash".
3. **Drive!** Take turns delivering imaginary pizzas, rescuing stuffed animals, or racing to the couch.
4. **Extend the world.** Let your child add a new stretch of road wherever they want — that's the best part.
