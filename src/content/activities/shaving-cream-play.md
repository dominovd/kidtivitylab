1. **Prep the zone.** Smock on the child, child in the high chair, cloth within your reach.
2. **Spray the mountain.** One fist-sized dollop in the middle of the tray.
3. **Let hands explore.** Squish, spread, pile — no goal, no instructions for the first minutes.
4. **Add one idea.** A finger road, a buried car, a drop of color, a letter to draw.
5. **Wipe-away finale.** Warm wet cloth over hands and tray — the whole scene dissolves in under a minute.
