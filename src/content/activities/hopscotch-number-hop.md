1. **Draw the grid.** Chalk a 1 to 10 hopscotch pattern on dry pavement, or tape it on a hard floor indoors. Mix single squares with side-by-side pairs, numbered 1 to 10.
2. **Set the throwing line.** Stand a few steps back and take turns tossing the beanbag onto a square. Whatever it lands on is out of bounds for hopping.
3. **Hop and call the numbers.** Hop on one foot on single squares, land with two feet on the side-by-side squares, and say each number out loud as you go.
4. **Turn around and pick it up.** At the top, hop back, skip the square with the beanbag, and scoop it up on the way past.
5. **Play a number round.** Call out a number and have your child hop straight to it. Try even numbers only, or two numbers in a row for a bigger challenge.
6. **Pass the beanbag.** Take turns until everyone has had a few rounds, then fold up the tape or let the chalk fade.
