1. **Make the friends.** Cut out (or print) ghosts, bats, cats and pumpkins, decorate if the crew insists.
2. **Hide them.** Tape friends around the house at pilot height while the witch waits.
3. **Preflight briefing.** Count the missing friends, mount the broom, review flight rules.
4. **Fly the rescue.** Gallop room to room, collect each friend with appropriate drama.
5. **Debrief at base.** Count the rescued, hear their stories, then swap: the child hides, you fly.
