1. **Collect the noisemakers.** Quietly gather 5–6 objects — keys, paper, a zipper, a cup and spoon — out of sight.
2. **Eyes closed.** The guesser shuts their eyes or snuggles under the blanket.
3. **Make one sound.** Jingle, crinkle, tap. One replay allowed on request.
4. **Guess and reveal.** Right or wrong, show the object and talk about what gave it away.
5. **Swap roles.** Now the child collects the sounds and stumps you — the best half of the game.
