1. **Start at A.** Find a license plate (or road sign) with the letter A on it.
2. **Work through the alphabet in order.** No skipping — that's where the challenge lives.
3. **Call it out.** "B! On the blue truck!" Everyone confirms.
4. **Stuck on Q or X?** Allow billboards and exit signs as backup — house rules are allowed.
