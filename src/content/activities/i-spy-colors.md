1. **Pick something you can both see.** "I spy something... red."
2. **Let them guess.** Wrong guesses get a warm "nope, keep looking!"
3. **Swap roles.** Their turn to spy is where the language practice really happens.
4. **In the car:** stick to colors of cars, signs, and trucks — things that stay visible for a while.
