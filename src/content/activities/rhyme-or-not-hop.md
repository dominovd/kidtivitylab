1. **Set the course.** Mark a start and a finish about 6 to 10 feet apart in a hallway or on the grass.
2. **Stand at the start.** Your child waits there while you stand nearby with your word pairs ready.
3. **Say two words.** "Cat, hat." Pause and let them think.
4. **Hop or freeze.** If the words rhyme, they hop forward once on one foot. If not, they hold a silly freeze pose.
5. **Keep going to the finish.** About 8 to 10 hops gets them there. Cheer at the line, then start over.
6. **Swap roles.** Let your child call the pairs and watch you hop or freeze.
