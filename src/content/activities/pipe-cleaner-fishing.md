1. **Make the fish.** Bend each pipe cleaner into a loop and twist the ends together. An adult folds any sharp tips inward.
2. **Fill a shallow pan.** An inch or two of water is plenty. Set a towel underneath.
3. **Drop the fish in.** Pipe cleaners soak up water and sink, so the catch waits on the bottom of the pan.
4. **Fish them out.** Start with hands, then offer tongs or a slotted spoon for a bigger challenge.
5. **Sort the catch.** Line the fish up on the towel or scoop each color into its own cup.
6. **Dry and store.** Pat the fish dry and keep them in a small bag for next time.
