1. **Choose the prompts.** Write familiar actions or animals on slips, or whisper one to each actor.
2. **First actor up.** They read or hear their word secretly and take the stage.
3. **Act, no words.** Body, face and mime only — guessers shout freely or by invisible microphone.
4. **Land the guess.** Correct answer becomes the next actor, or teams score a point.
5. **Keep turns moving.** After everyone has acted, finish with reverse charades if the group wants one lively final round.
