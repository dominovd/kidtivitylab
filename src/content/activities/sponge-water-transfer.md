1. **Set up two bowls.** Put an inch of water in one bowl and leave the other empty. Place both on a towel.
2. **Show the squeeze.** Soak the sponge, lift it, and squeeze it slowly into the empty bowl while your child watches.
3. **Hand it over.** Give your child the sponge and let them try. Stay close for under-3s.
4. **Let them repeat.** Fill, lift, squeeze, and watch the bowls slowly swap. Refill as needed.
5. **Add a tool.** Bring in a baster or small cup for a new way to move the water.
6. **Empty and dry.** Pour the water out right after play and wring the sponge dry.
