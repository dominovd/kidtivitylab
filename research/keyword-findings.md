# Kidtivitylab — Keyword Research Findings (US, Semrush broad match, Jul 26, 2026)

Dataset: "activities for" (10,003 kws) + "indoor games" (1,811 kws), deduped, volume > 0 → 10,354 keywords, ~541K combined monthly US searches.

## Headline numbers
- Low-competition niche: ~84% of KD-rated volume sits at KD ≤ 30. 508 keywords with KD ≤ 25 and vol ≥ 100 (~200K/mo combined).
- Age is the primary axis: 5,668 age-specific keywords (~204K vol). Peak at ages 2–3 (35.8K and 28.3K), then 4 (20K), 5 (21K), 1 (13K). Demand drops sharply after age 6. Bonus: "X month old" (babies) — 878 kws, 32K vol.
- Situational clusters beat generic themes: rainy day — 65K vol (top keyword "rainy day activities for kids": 60,500/mo, KD 12); holidays & seasons — 64K (Christmas 8K, Easter 7.3K, Halloween 7.2K, fall 4.7K; KD 7–19); indoor — 43.5K; learning/STEM — 32K; crafts — 15.5K; outdoor — 15K; birthday/party — 12K; sensory — 6.4K (KD 13.6); car/travel — 5K (KD 9).
- "Near me" (32K vol) = local intent, not addressable — excluded.

## SERP risks & signals
- AI Overview: on 13% of keywords but 61% of volume — head terms are exposed. Long tail (age × situation) + the interactive tool are the defensible play.
- Video SERP features on 70% of volume — short activity demo clips are a ranking asset.
- Printables cluster has the highest KD (~30, Pinterest & worksheet sites) — use printables as lead magnets, not as an SEO bet.

## Recommended page matrix (v1)
1. Age hubs (backbone): /age/2-year-olds/ … ages 1–10 + months for babies.
2. Age × situation: indoor, rainy day, outdoor, car/road trip, sick day.
3. Age × goal/type: sensory, learning/STEM, crafts, games.
4. Seasonal/holiday: christmas, easter, halloween, fall, winter, summer (+ age). Publish 2–3 months before peak.
5. Tool (Activity Finder) on home & hubs; printables as in-page lead magnets.

Launch priority: rainy day + indoor + age hubs 1–5 — maximum volume at KD 9–25.
